Hangover Ramen

Description

I made this based on a "You Suck at Cooking" video, about a number of different instant ramen modifications. This one caught my eye since it used cheese which I had never had before with instant noodles. It was a disaster.

I had to make a bunch of modifications myself, like I didn't even have parmesan at the time so I used mozzarella. After eating this all I felt was I'd be better off having sleep for dinner. But this was one of the first times I ever tried doing something different with instant noodles, I hadn't even added an egg with noodles before this. And the biggest lesson I learned from making this was not to give up on a dish just because it wasn't good from the get go. And send it out even when it isn't ready, ESPECIALLY if it isn't ready. I made this again later and it turned out better. I've made several different types of these cheesy ramen recipes, which I've dubbed "poor man's carbonara", but sometimes I like looking back on this one after having something I really like, and remind myself that I had to start off with a dish I couldn't stomach before I could make dishes I heavily enjoy without even trying at this point.
